How to make the sound /ɪ/

**Mục A10a:**

* /ɪ/ is a short sound.


* Look at the diagram.


* Listen and then say the sound.


* Make your mouth a bit less wide than for /iː/.


* Your tongue is a bit further back in your mouth than for /iː/.


* Target sound: /ɪ/
